Every journey begins somewhere, and in romantic love, that beginning is often marked by an undeniable surge of emotion. Modern science tells us that when we first feel drawn to someone, our brains release a cocktail of hormones—dopamine, oxytocin, and serotonin. This biological rush creates a sense of euphoria, making the world seem brighter and the other person seem almost flawless.

This phase, often referred to as infatuation or the "honeymoon period," is nature’s way of drawing two strangers together. It is thrilling, electric, and beautiful. However, this intense spark is only the doorway to the house of love, not the house itself.

Key Insight: Passion is the spark that lights the fire, but it takes patience, effort, and depth to keep the flame burning when the initial rush fades.
